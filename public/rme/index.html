You didn't build your business on someone else's promises, and you won't buy solar on ours. Here's the math that owners across Placer, Sacramento, and Yuba counties are acting on — and what it fixes.

Rates you can't forecast

Commercial electric rates in Northern California have climbed year after year, and demand charges punish the exact hours you operate. Solar converts a volatile operating expense into a fixed, financeable asset with a known cost per kilowatt-hour for 25+ years.

→ Budget certainty you can put in next year's plan

Capital that has to perform

The 30% federal Investment Tax Credit plus accelerated depreciation typically returns a substantial share of system cost in the first years. Most commercial systems we design pay for themselves well inside their warranty period — then produce for decades.

→ An IRR you can compare against any other use of capital

A roof that earns nothing

Your roofline, carport, or yard is square footage you already pay to insure and maintain. Panels turn it into generation capacity that raises property value and tenant appeal without taking a single parking space out of service.

→ Dead square footage becomes a producing asset

Customers asking about sustainability

Corporate buyers and municipal contracts increasingly ask for sustainability credentials. On-site generation is the most visible, verifiable answer there is — and it's one you only pay for once.

→ ESG answers backed by your own meter data

RESIDENTIAL + BATTERY

When the grid goes down, your lights don't have to.

If you live between the valley and the foothills, you know the drill — heat waves, red-flag warnings, public safety power shutoffs. A solar and battery system from RME keeps the refrigerator cold, the well pump running, and the Wi-Fi on while you lock in what you pay for power.

  • Bill certainty. Swap a rising utility bill for a fixed cost you control.
  • Outage resilience. Battery backup carries your essential circuits, automatically.
  • EV ready. Charge on sunshine instead of peak rates.
  • Home value. An owned system raises resale value — and the warranty transfers.
